# Learning goal

This is a portfolio project **and** a learning project. The owner is preparing for senior full-stack interviews (Angular + .NET) and must be able to explain every part of the code.

## How to explain things

- Plain words first. Do not assume prior knowledge.
- Order: **what it is, how it works, why it is like that, when and where to use it.**
- Include a small diagram (ASCII is fine) and a concrete example from this app.
- Name the trade-off and the alternative that was rejected.
- End a feature with interview material: 6 to 10 likely questions with short model answers, 3 teach-back questions for the owner to answer, and one paper or hands-on exercise.

## What to produce for each finished spec

`ai/learning/<spec-id>.md` with: what we built, how it works (diagram), why these choices, when to use or avoid them, interview Q&A, teach-back questions, exercise. Also give a short version in the chat.

## Working with the owner

- Keep answers simple and step by step, especially for tool setup on Windows.
- If a step may fail, say what error to expect and what it means.
- Never hide an uncertainty. If code was not compiled or run, say so.
- Do not do the owner's teach-back or exercise for them.
